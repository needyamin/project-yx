1. Overview & Principles

Welcome to Project YX ("we", "our", or "the Application"), published by ANSNEW TECH. Project YX is a high-performance desktop video editor engineered for Windows and Linux.

Our core design philosophy is simple: your creative work and personal data belong exclusively to you. Project YX operates as an offline-first, standalone desktop application. We do not operate remote user tracking servers, cloud databases of user videos, or analytics pipelines.

✓
Clear Privacy Guarantee: When you use Project YX, your media files, timelines, project files, and personal identifiers never leave your computer unless you explicitly choose to share or upload them elsewhere using your own independent tools.

3. Your Media & Local Files

To function as a video editor, Project YX requires standard operating system permissions to read and write files on your local drive:

  • Importing Media: When you drag-and-drop or select video, audio, or image files, the application reads them from your local storage to generate preview frames and waveform visualizers.
  • Saving Project Files: Timeline data, cuts, crop boxes, and project metadata are saved as local configuration files on your machine.
  • Exporting Media: Final rendered video outputs (MP4, WebM, etc.) are written directly to your chosen local folder or output destination.
ℹ
Project YX never transmits your source media or exported videos across the internet. All transcoding, scaling, and video rendering are executed locally on your hardware.

4. Network & Internet Access

Project YX can function entirely without an active internet connection. Any internet connectivity is strictly limited to user-initiated or explicit actions:

  • Software Update Checks (Optional): The application may query the public GitHub Release API (or Microsoft Store update services) to inform you when a newer version of the software is available. No user data, files, or tracking tokens are sent during this query.
  • External Documentation Links: If you click a link within the application to view the user manual, source code, or issue tracker, your default web browser will open that destination URL (e.g., GitHub). These external websites are governed by their respective privacy policies.

5. Third-Party Services

Project YX does not integrate advertising SDKs, third-party analytics trackers (such as Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or Amplitude), or commercial data-sharing plugins.

If acquired through the Microsoft Store, the installation and licensing verification processes are managed by Microsoft Corporation in accordance with the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

6. Storage, Retention & Security

Because we do not collect or store your data on external servers:

  • Data Retention: You retain complete custody of your projects and media. Files remain on your computer for as long as you choose to keep them.
  • Data Deletion: You can delete any project file, cache directory, or exported video at any time through standard operating system file management. Uninstalling the application will remove application binaries.
  • Security: The application leverages sandboxed desktop runtime architectures (Tauri) with restricted native capabilities, ensuring the app operates safely within your operating system environment.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Project YX is a general-purpose desktop utility and does not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in any jurisdiction), in full compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international privacy laws.

8. Your Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA / CPRA)

International privacy frameworks (such as the European General Data Protection Regulation and California Consumer Privacy Act) grant individuals specific rights regarding their personal data, including the right to access, rectify, port, and erase their information.

Because Project YX collects no personal data and maintains no central registry of users, we have no personal information to provide, sell, delete, or monetize. You have full, direct sovereign control over all your data directly on your device.
